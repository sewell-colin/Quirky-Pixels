About the artwork

Quirky Pixels is original, humorous digital artwork with a twist. The images cover a range of genres and topics - often with a bizarre or surreal sense of humour.

All images are based on original ideas by the artist and are copyright Quirky Pixels. Each image is unique and never duplicated.

The images are available to purchase - in which case you own the unique rights for the image and can use it in whatever way you wish. If you are interested then see here for more information.

The artwork is also available for use in your own work or projects if you first notify the artist and ask permission. See here for details.

About the artist

Despite never having the skill or ability to 'draw' in the traditional sense using pencils or paintbrushes, Colin has always liked and appreciated art. Not to be defeated, he decided to use his IT and software skills to express his creative streak and sense of humour.

Using a mixture of language to 'play on words', modern digital technology and cloud services, and image manipulation software, Colin creates unique, original, humorous and sometimes surreal and unconventional images that challenge our notion of traditional art and subject matters.

He hopes you enjoy the artwork and ideas as much as he has enjoyed creating them.
